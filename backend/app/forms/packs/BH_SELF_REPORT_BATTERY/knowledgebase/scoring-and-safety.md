# Scoring And Safety Notes

Scores are screening summaries, not diagnoses.

Safety-sensitive items:

- PHQ-9 item 9 and DSM-5 Level 1 self-harm item are frequency items. Any answer
  above "Not at all" should be visible in review.
- C-SSRS positive intent, plan, or behavior answers should be flagged as high
  risk for prompt safety follow-up.

Scoring overview:

- PHQ-9: nine items scored 0 to 3, total 0 to 27.
- GAD-7: seven items scored 0 to 3, total 0 to 21.
- AUDIT-C: three items scored 0 to 4, total 0 to 12.
- DAST-10: yes/no drug-use problem items, total 0 to 10. The item about being
  able to stop is reverse scored.
- PCL-5: twenty items scored 0 to 4, total 0 to 80.
- MDQ: positive-screen pattern uses seven or more yes symptom items, symptoms
  occurring in the same time period, and moderate or serious problems.
- WHODAS 2.0 12-item: simple scoring sums twelve 0 to 4 ratings and also reports
  a transformed 0 to 100 score.
- DLA-20 self-report functioning in this pack uses twenty 1 to 5 self-ratings
  and reports a transformed 0 to 100 difficulty score.

The review page and clinical export should display scores with the tool name,
completion status, total score, maximum score, and interpretation.
