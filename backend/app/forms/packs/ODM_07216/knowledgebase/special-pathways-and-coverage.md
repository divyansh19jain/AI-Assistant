# ODM 07216 Special Pathways and Coverage Guidance

Audience: AI voice assistant explaining special Medicaid paths and coverage
questions while filling ODM 07216.

Keywords: pregnancy, pregnant, postpartum, baby, medical bills, retroactive,
last 3 months, citizen, US national, naturalized, immigration, eligible
immigration status, document type, veteran, military, foster care, incarcerated,
aged, blind, disabled, disability, daily activities, in-home care, nursing home,
long-term care, Appendix E, Medicare, Medicare Premium Assistance, QMB, SLMB,
QI-1, current insurance, employer insurance, COBRA, retiree, TRICARE, VA.

## Pregnancy and recent pregnancy

ODM 07216 asks whether a person is currently pregnant, how many babies are
expected, the expected due date if known, whether the person was recently
pregnant, how many babies were expected, and when the pregnancy ended. Pregnancy
can change the Medicaid pathway and household-size screening, so collect these
answers carefully.

Voice phrasing: "Pregnancy can affect which Medicaid path applies, so I need to
ask this directly. Are you currently pregnant?"

## Medical bills from the last three months

The form asks whether the person wants help paying for medical bills from the
last three months. The assistant should explain this as possible retroactive
help for recent medical bills, not as a promise that the bills will be paid.

Voice phrasing: "This asks whether you want Medicaid to look back at medical
bills from the last three months. It does not guarantee payment, but it lets the
state review it."

## Citizenship and immigration

ODM 07216 asks whether a person applying for coverage is a U.S. citizen or U.S.
national. If not, it asks whether the person has eligible immigration status and
then asks for document type and document ID number.

The form also says families that include non-citizens can apply. A parent or
other household member may apply for a child even if that adult is not applying
for coverage. Applying for a child should not be described as changing the adult's
citizenship or permanent-resident chances.

Voice phrasing: "Families with non-citizens can still apply. I only need
immigration document details for people who are asking for coverage and have
that status."

## ABD and long-term care triggers

The form asks whether a person has a physical, mental, or emotional condition
that limits daily activities, and whether the person needs in-home care or is in
a nursing home or long-term care facility. A "yes" can mean Appendix E is needed.

Appendix E is for Medicaid for the Aged, Blind, or Disabled, help with Medicare
premiums, or in-home/nursing facility care. The assistant should explain this
plainly and continue gathering the form.

Voice phrasing: "If you say yes, the application may need extra Appendix E
questions. I will keep track of that and help you continue."

## Medicare Premium Assistance

The Medicare Premium Assistance Programs may help people who are eligible for
Medicare and have limited income or assets with Medicare premiums, deductibles,
co-pays, or coinsurance. If the person asks about QMB, SLMB, or QI-1, explain
that these are Medicare premium help categories and continue the application.

Do not decide final MPAP eligibility. Use the official 2026 income guide only as
screening and keep collecting required fields.

## Current health coverage

Step 5 asks about current coverage for anyone who needs health coverage:
Medicaid, Medicare, VA health care, Peace Corps, TRICARE, employer insurance,
and other coverage. It also asks about job-based coverage that was offered in
the past three months but not taken.

Voice phrasing: "Having insurance does not automatically mean you should stop.
The form still asks because there may be lower-cost or better coverage options."

## Foster care, incarceration, military, and other-state benefits

The form asks about foster care after age 18, current incarceration, military
service, and whether the person currently or previously received benefits in
another state. Ask these as neutral questions. Do not imply the answer is bad.

Voice phrasing: "These questions help route the application correctly. A yes
does not mean we stop."
