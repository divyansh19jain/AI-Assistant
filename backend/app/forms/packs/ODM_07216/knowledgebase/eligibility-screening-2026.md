# Ohio Medicaid Eligibility Screening Guide, 2026

Audience: AI voice assistant helping an Ohio resident complete ODM 07216.

Use this as screening guidance only. The assistant may say, "Based on the
official Ohio guidance, this looks like it may be under/over one income path,"
but must not say the person is finally eligible or ineligible. County Job and
Family Services and Ohio Medicaid make the final decision after the application
and any verification are reviewed. If the facts are uncertain, keep helping the
person apply.

Keywords: qualify, eligible, eligibility, income limit, too much income, Federal
Poverty Level, FPL, MAGI, adult, child, pregnant, parent, caretaker, Medicare
premium, QMB, SLMB, QI-1, MBIWD, Ohio WorkAbility.

## Basic screening posture

- Encourage the person to apply even if they are not sure. ODM 07216 says the
  application is used to see what coverage or help paying costs the person may
  qualify for.
- Do not discourage an application because income seems high. Other facts can
  matter, including household size, category, pregnancy, disability, age,
  Medicare status, deductions, expenses, medical bills, immigration status, and
  whether a child has other insurance.
- If the person asks, "Do I qualify?", answer in plain language: "I can help you
  compare your household and income to the official guide, but the state makes
  the final decision. Let's gather the details so the application is complete."

## 2026 monthly income guidelines for common MAGI categories

Effective March 1, 2026, ODM MEPL 194 lists monthly Federal Poverty Level
guidelines for MAGI-based Medicaid. The chart below is a screening aid. Use the
person's household size and likely category, then continue the application.

Family size 1:
- Parent/caretaker relative, 90% FPL: $1,197
- MAGI adult age 19-64, 133% FPL: $1,769
- Child with creditable insurance, 156% FPL: $2,075
- Pregnant woman, 200% FPL: $2,660
- Child without creditable insurance, 206% FPL: $2,740

Family size 2:
- Parent/caretaker relative: $1,623
- MAGI adult age 19-64: $2,399
- Child with creditable insurance: $2,814
- Pregnant woman: $3,607
- Child without creditable insurance: $3,715

Family size 3:
- Parent/caretaker relative: $2,049
- MAGI adult age 19-64: $3,028
- Child with creditable insurance: $3,552
- Pregnant woman: $4,554
- Child without creditable insurance: $4,690

Family size 4:
- Parent/caretaker relative: $2,475
- MAGI adult age 19-64: $3,658
- Child with creditable insurance: $4,290
- Pregnant woman: $5,500
- Child without creditable insurance: $5,665

Family size 5:
- Parent/caretaker relative: $2,901
- MAGI adult age 19-64: $4,288
- Child with creditable insurance: $5,029
- Pregnant woman: $6,447
- Child without creditable insurance: $6,641

Family size 6:

For family sizes 7-12, use the official ODM chart. If the assistant does not
have the exact row available, say that and continue gathering the application.

## How to explain income limits in voice

Good: "For a household of three, the 2026 monthly guide shows $3,028 for many
adults age 19 to 64. That is only a screening guide. Let's still finish the
application because other rules may apply."

Good: "Pregnancy has a different income path than a non-pregnant adult, so I need
to collect the pregnancy questions before we compare income."

Avoid: "You are not eligible."

Avoid: "You should not apply."

## Medicare Premium Assistance and MBIWD

MEPL 194 also lists 2026 Medicare Premium Assistance Program guidelines:
- QMB, 100% FPL: family size 1 $1,330; family size 2 $1,804.
- SLMB, 120% FPL: family size 1 $1,596; family size 2 $2,164.
- QI-1, 135% FPL: family size 1 $1,796; family size 2 $2,435.

For Medicaid Buy-In for Workers with Disabilities / Ohio WorkAbility, MEPL 194
lists a 250% FPL individual income guideline of $3,325.
